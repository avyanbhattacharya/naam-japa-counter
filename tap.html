Free online Japa counter and digital mala

The Tap Japa Counter gives you a large +1 button for manually counting each naam or mantra repetition. Every tap updates your total, completed rounds of 108, and progress through the current round.

Your count is saved automatically on the device. You can close or refresh the page and continue later. For a completely hands-free practice, switch to the Touchless Japa Counter with adjustable automatic counting.

How to use the tap counter

Tap the large button once for every naam. One completed round is recorded after 108 taps. The progress indicator shows how many repetitions remain in the active round. Use Reset only when you want to begin a new session.

Private and lightweight

This digital Japa counter runs locally in your browser without an account, advertisements, analytics, or external dependencies. Your count is not sent to a server. Clearing this browser’s site data will remove the saved count.

Frequently asked questions

Does each tap count as one naam?

Yes. Each press of the large TAP +1 button increases the total by exactly one.

How does the counter track Japa rounds?

The counter treats every 108 taps as one completed round and automatically starts progress toward the next round.

Will my count remain after closing the page?

Yes. The count is saved in local browser storage on the same device unless you reset it or clear the browser’s site data.

Is this counter free and ad-free?

Yes. The counter is free to use and contains no advertisements or tracking analytics.
