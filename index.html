Free automatic Japa counter

The Touchless Japa Counter increases the naam count automatically at the pace you choose. It displays your total count, completed rounds of 108, progress through the current round, and elapsed practice time.

Set a speed from one to five seconds per naam, press Start, and continue your practice without touching the screen. If you prefer to count each repetition yourself, use the Tap Japa Counter with a large +1 button.

How the touchless counter works

The counter uses real elapsed time instead of depending only on a browser timer. On an iPhone, the visible number may pause while the screen is locked, but it catches up when you return. Your speed changes, count, and elapsed time are saved locally in your browser.

Privacy and simplicity

This lightweight Japa counter has no advertisements, accounts, analytics, or external dependencies. Your Japa count is not uploaded to a server. Clearing this browser’s site data will also clear the saved session.

Frequently asked questions

What is one round?

For this counter, one round equals 108 naam. After every 108 counts, the completed-round total increases automatically.

Can I change the counting speed?

Yes. Use the slider to select a pace from one to five seconds per naam. You can adjust it before or during a session without losing your existing count.

Does it work when the iPhone screen locks?

The web page cannot update visibly while iOS suspends it, but the counter calculates the missed interval from the clock and displays the correct total when you reopen it.

Is the Japa counter free?

Yes. Both the automatic and tap counters are free, ad-free, and run entirely in your browser.
